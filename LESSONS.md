2026-10-13 [dev] fake-service-overwritten-by-platform-load — `test_a_new_session_restores_the_base_before_anything_is_forced` failed about every other run (T-068): the fake inverter registered `select.select_option` before the entry was set up, the restore run starts in `async_start` before the platforms are forwarded, and loading the `select`/`number` domains registered the real entity services over the fake ones, so the write went to an entity-less service (`write_failed`). A test whose first run starts during entry setup must set up the domains whose services it fakes first (`install_inverter`); look at the debug log for "Referenced entities … are missing" before suspecting the engine, and never fix such a flake with a retry.
2026-10-13 [all] red-ci-on-a-diff-that-cannot-cause-it — the PR run of T-474 (LESSONS.md + one ADR) failed `tests/test_controller_native.py::test_a_new_session_restores_the_base_before_anything_is_forced` while the push run of the same sha was green and the test passed 25/25 locally in the CI image. Before debugging a red PR, compare the push and pull_request runs of the same sha and the branch diff; a failure the diff cannot reach gets its own task with the run ids and the evidence (T-478), not a fix or a skip inside the unrelated branch.
2026-10-13 [architect/dev] planner-bound-reused-as-bill-base — the year oracle (T-460) took the month's energy part from `buy_energy`, a field the engine clamps at 0 per hour for the planning price, and copied that bound into the tool input; the statute nets hours inside the month, so every month with a draw at a negative energy price was overstated and the negative-month rule of T-473 was unreachable from the CLI (T-474, ADR-0001 Amendment T-474). A quantity a bill sums is specified from the settlement rule (sign, period, what nets with what), not from the planner's input contract; when one field feeds both the planner and a bill, write the range each consumer needs and convert at the boundary.
2026-10-13 [architect/dev] owner-decision-left-in-the-queue — the owner accepted the CHARGE_PV solve-time cost in T-021, the answer stayed in the queue, and ADR-0006 then called the same regression "not accepted"; T-039 and T-044 re-decided it. The question had also promised an optimisation in a task (T-011) that had already merged, and the 4-7x came from one regular benchmark (1.3-1.6x on a random corpus, where the rule also changes the plan cost). An owner decision that changes what the product accepts is written into the docs it touches (ADR or `docs/model.md`, with the task id and the numbers shown to the owner) in the PR it unblocks; before writing "open" or "not accepted", search the queue for a DECIDE task on the subject; never offer the owner a follow-up without checking that the task is still open and covers it.
2026-10-13 [dev/architect] rule-moved-rows-into-a-branch-that-lacks-a-later-rule — T-038 widened the idle set (guarded and probe rows) and was rebased onto a `main` where ADR-0005 had added CURTAIL to the ordinary committed branch of `constrain_modes` only; the idle branches offered HOLD alone, four problems planned on `main` had no plan, and the ADR evidence still named the pre-rebase `main` (review round 4, after three rounds each finding one neighbour gap). When a change routes rows into another branch of the model, list per branch what `main` offers those rows (modes, releases) and offer the same or record why not; after every rebase that brings engine changes, re-run the head-vs-`main` sweep against the new `main` with a generator aimed at the rule that landed, compare bridges as well as modes, and name the `main` SHA in the evidence.
2026-10-12 [architect] conclusion-from-a-partial-corpus — T-449's first 17 of 40 year-runs (two operators) put `cost_min` on G12 net-metering at a median of −38 PLN/yr against self-use; all 20 G12 homes gave +21, and `self_sufficiency` "never better" was 7-9 PLN of energy still in the battery on the last day. Decide on the whole planned corpus, split a cost difference into its parts (bill, fixed fees, wear, year-end stored energy) before reading its sign, and log partial results as partial.
2026-10-12 [architect] objective-change-accepted-on-a-worked-example — ADR-0001 rev 2 (deposit factors) was accepted and implemented on one worked example; a one-year replay valued by the bill (T-448) showed π = 1 dearer in 40 of 40 RCE homes and no setting better than `off` by more than 44 PLN/yr. A change to planning prices, the objective or a profile amount ships with a year replay of the real planner against the previous behaviour, valued by the bill (annual formula and monthly ledger), numbers in the ADR. A measurement from a port counts only at the planner's own cadence: plan-once-a-day with the plan's tail executed inflated the loss 2–4× and invented one on RCEm.
2026-10-12 [dev/architect] tolerance-claim-from-one-benchmark — T-022 proposed a 3e-3 MIP gap from one synthetic problem, labelled the baseline "1e-4" (the solve already ran at gap 0) and quoted the change in money while the gap bounds the weighted objective (ADR-0003: up to 9.6 % of cost on a random corpus).
